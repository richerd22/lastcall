    Quick answers

    How late are bars open in Chicago?

    Standard tavern and consumption licenses close at 2 a.m., or 3 a.m. early Sunday. A late-hour license, which needs neighbor notice and sometimes a majority petition of nearby voters, allows 4 a.m., or 5 a.m. early Sunday.
